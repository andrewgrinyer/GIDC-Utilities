HOW A LEAD IS MATCHED
   Email first. If no email matches, the phone (last ten digits). If neither, the full name,
   and only within a year of the lead.
   Sold: a sale on or after the day the lead came in. Sold Price is the price on the estimate
   in Access (Estimates.Price) for the first sale after the lead.
   Booked: not sold, but an estimate was made in Access from two weeks before the lead onward
   (the office may have taken their call first).
   Not Booked: neither. A past customer from years ago who writes in again stays Not Booked
   until the office makes them a new estimate.
   Dealer and wholesale jobs are left out.
