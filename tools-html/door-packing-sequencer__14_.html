Door Packing Sequencer

Drop in the order spreadsheets for a load. Doors are merged and sequenced into one bottom-to-top stacking order, ready to export for the packing station.

Sequencing rules
  1. Tallest heights on the bottom
  2. Within a height, widest widths first
  3. Within a width, one solid core sits on top
  4. Any other solid cores sit on the bottom

How it reads your files: the tool finds the door rows on the order's upload sheet and pulls location, quantity, door type, style, opening height & width, paint and customer. Core is read from the product type — Hollow = HC (hollow core), Solid / Thick = SC (solid core).
Height bands set the stacking layers, bottom to top: 90–96, 84–90, then 84 and under — measured on the finished height. Within a band, widest first; then one solid core rides on top of each width and any others go on the bottom.
Panel H & Panel W are the finished door dimensions. Height: bypass doors deduct 3.35 from the opening height; bifold and pivot doors deduct 1.5. Width: a double bifold, double swing or bypass divides the opening width by its panel count; single bifolds, pocket and barn doors keep their full width.
Panels & labels. Every panel is its own pallet level, tagged on the opening number: a single bifold stays one row marked a/b (two panels side by side); a double bifold splits into a/b and c/d; a double swing into A (active) and P (passive); bypass panels run A, B, C…; pocket and pivot panels are A, B. Total panels count a single bifold as 2 and a double bifold as 4. Grouping is global across every order in the load, so one stack covers the whole truck.
